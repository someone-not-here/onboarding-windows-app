## Research and Learn

### What are the main Agile ceremonies, and what is their purpose?
- **Daily Stand-ups**: Short meetings (usually ~15 min.) where team members share what they've worked on, any blockers they've had, and what they plan to do next.
- **Sprint Planning (Scrum) vs. Continuous Prioritization (Kanban)**
    - *Scrum:* Teams plan out fixed sets of work with defined goals (sprints).
    - *Kanban:* Work is continuously prioritized without fixed sprints.
- **Retrospectives**: Meetings for team members to reflect on what went well, what didn't, how to improve.
- **Backlog Refinement**: Sessions where the team or product owner clarify, estimate, and prioritize items within the backlog.

### How do Agile teams collaborate asynchronously and across time zones?
Collaboration is done through written updates, task boards, documentation, and recorded, online meetings. These allow asynchronous teams to be on the same page despite their different schedules.

## Reflection

### How do Agile ceremonies help with communication and alignment?
Agile ceremonies provide a structured, regular cadence for the team to share progress, adapt to changes, maintain transparency, and align on priorities. They help reduce misunderstandings and keep the team focused on common goals.

### Which ceremony do you think is most important for your role, and why?
I think daily stand-ups are the most important as team members can identify any blockers they have early on. They also give other team members insight into what others are currently working on and what their plans are. This ensures cohesion with the team.
